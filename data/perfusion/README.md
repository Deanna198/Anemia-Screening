# Perfusion experiment — how to record

**Question:** does the inner eyelid change colour when blood flow changes, even though Hb has not changed?

**Volunteers:** 8–15 healthy adults (teammates, friends). Informal, no blood draw, no names stored.
Ask your guide whether the department needs a consent note; a one-line verbal consent is usually enough for
photos of the eyelid with no identifying details.

**Same for every photo:** same phone, same room, same lighting (daylight from a window, no direct sun),
**flash off**, phone about 10–15 cm from the eye, gently pull the lower eyelid down, tap to focus on the eyelid.

| Photo | When | File name |
|---|---|---|
| Rest | after 5 minutes sitting quietly | `v01_rest.jpg` |
| After | straight after 30 seconds of squats, **or** after lying down 5 minutes and standing up | `v01_after.jpg` |
| Recovery (optional) | 5 minutes later, sitting again | `v01_recovery.jpg` |

Use `v02_…`, `v03_…` for the next volunteers. Take 2–3 shots each time and keep the sharpest.
Copy the photos into this folder and run `python src/perfusion/perfusion_analysis.py`.
